Reading the plots

Valence runs left to right, arousal bottom to top. The dashed ring is where the brief aimed. The hollow dot is the first candidate, the filled dot is the best one the optimiser found, and the line between them is how far it travelled. Axes span ±0.40 of the ±1 circumplex — the region this synthesiser can actually reach.
