  estimator_B    HV_HA 48   HV_LA 0   LV_HA 0   LV_LA 0

An estimator concentrated in one quadrant inflates raw agreement with
everything else, which is why kappa is reported alongside it.

pair                                raw   chance    kappa
estimator_A2 vs estimator_B2      33.3%    21.0%   +0.157
estimator_A2 vs estimator_B       20.8%    20.8%   +0.000
estimator_B2 vs estimator_B       10.4%    10.4%   +0.000

kappa near 0 means the estimators agree no more than their own marginal
distributions would produce by chance. Negative means less than chance.
The usual reference bands (Landis & Koch, 1977) are conventions rather than
thresholds: <=0.20 slight, 0.21-0.40 fair, 0.41-0.60 moderate,
0.61-0.80 substantial, >0.80 almost perfect.

Low agreement means the estimators disagree about where these stimuli sit,
not that one of them is wrong. Nothing here establishes which is closer to
what a listener would report. A pair involving an estimator with almost no
spread should be read as a discrimination failure, not as disagreement.
