Feature guide (acoustic meaning only; these are not emotion labels):
- Tempo (BPM): estimated pulse rate in beats per minute.
- Mode: estimated major or minor tonal mode.
- Mean pitch (MIDI): average detected fundamental pitch.
- Pitch slope (MIDI/s): average pitch direction; positive values rise and negative values fall.
- Spectral centroid (Hz): centre of spectral energy; larger values indicate a brighter spectrum.
- RMS level (dBFS): overall signal level; values closer to 0 dBFS indicate greater signal level.
- Onset rate (per s): detected note/event onsets per second.
- Dynamic range (dB): spread between quieter and louder frames.
- Calibration percentile: relative position of that acoustic measurement within an independent target-blind synthetic calibration set; it carries no emotion label.
