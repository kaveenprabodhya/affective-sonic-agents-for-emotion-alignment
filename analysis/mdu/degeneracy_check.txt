READING THIS TABLE

Stress-1 alone cannot separate a good solution from a degenerate one: a
degenerate solution achieves LOW stress precisely by collapsing. The CV of
the fitted distances is what distinguishes them. When it approaches zero,
every persona sits at almost the same distance from every emotion term, so
the configuration carries no information whatever its stress value.

smacof minimises a penalised stress (Busing, Groenen & Heiser, 2005) whose
penalty exists to prevent this. omega sets its strength; omega = 0 removes
it. The ordinal rows below therefore show what the transformation does with
and without that protection.
