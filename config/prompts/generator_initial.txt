Melodic and rhythmic design guidance:

- contour controls the broad melodic trajectory across the complete sonic logo:
  rising, falling, arch, flat, or wave.

- motif_pattern controls the local interval movement around that broad contour:
  - stepwise: neighbouring scale movement around the contour.
  - skip_return: a moderate leap away from an anchor followed by a return.
  - thirds: alternating third-like interval movement.
  - anchor_leap: stronger leaps away from and back to an anchor pitch.
  - zigzag: alternating upward and downward interval movement.
  - arpeggio: chord-like expansion around the contour.

- rhythm_pattern controls temporal phrasing, independently of the melodic motif:
  - even uses regularly spaced note onsets.
  - short_short_long groups two shorter intervals followed by a longer interval.
  - long_short_short begins with a longer interval followed by two shorter intervals.
  - syncopated alternates shorter and longer onset intervals.
  - pickup_resolution begins with short pickup intervals and ends each phrase with a longer interval.
  - cadential groups several shorter intervals before a longer closing interval.
